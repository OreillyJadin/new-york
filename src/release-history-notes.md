## Conflicts and caveats

- **Golden Diner day count:** Resy's current guide says “30 days in advance at midnight.” One guide notes that Resy's 30-date visible calendar may count the current date, resulting in the newest displayed date being 29 calendar days away. The date above uses literal 30-day subtraction; check the Resy calendar at the edge of the window, including the prior date, rather than treating the counting convention as a confirmed policy change.
- **Tavern on the Green:** Its official site directs holiday reservations to phone at any time of year. That does not match assuming a standard online booking drop.
- **SUMMIT and Rockefeller:** The official calendars returned disabled dates / no event rows for the checked target date. Those are unavailable results, not sellout evidence. The saved screenshots are `shots/summit-jan-2027-calendar.png` and `shots/rockefeller-rink-jan-2027-calendar.png`.
- **Din Tai Fung walk-ins:** Reports differ by time: Eater's July 2024 opening coverage said the NYC location did not take walk-ins, while the current official FAQ offers an on-site waitlist. The FAQ is the current policy; no typical wait duration was established.
- **River Café:** The browser loaded OpenTable, but the target-date selection showed no visible slot list. No booking was made and no Notify action was used.
- **Resy/OpenTable limitations:** Resy's `/4/find` endpoint had returned HTTP 403 in earlier checks; OpenTable automated searches had been blocked. Browser checks were read-only. No blocked or empty result was labeled sold out.

Evidence URLs, short source quotations, and source dates are recorded per item in `release-history.json`. Existing `bookings.json`, `bookings-notes.md`, and HTML/todo files were not changed.
