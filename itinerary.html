What to wear

Early January in NYC usually means highs in the high 30s °F and lows in the 20s, with wind on the ferries, the bridge and the Summit terrace. Check the forecast the week before.

Every day
Warm coat, hat, scarf, gloves, layers, and comfortable waterproof walking shoes. You'll walk 15–20k steps on Jan 2 and Jan 3.
Bond 45 (NYE)
Festive and dressed up but not formal: cocktail dress or nice pants, and a blazer for him. You'll walk there in the cold, so bring a coat you don't mind checking.
Stella 34
Casual. You'll be there between Macy's and the tree, so dress for walking in the cold.
Ice rink
Gloves (required in practice), thick socks, pants rather than a skirt.
SUMMIT
The floors are mirrored, so wear pants or tights rather than a short skirt or dress. Avoid stilettos and steel-toe boots, which can scratch the mirrors.
Rockettes
Dressy casual.
Carbone
No shorts, open-toed shoes or tank tops. Dressy is the norm.
St. Patrick's Mass
Modest: covered shoulders, nothing too casual.
River Café
Jackets required for men. Dressy for both.
